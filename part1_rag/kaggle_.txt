Answer questions over three economics PDFs, with figures, conflicts and conversations
Overview (Description)
Build a multimodal retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) system that answers questions about three economics PDF documents using their text, figures and tables.

The three documents are a read-only knowledge base. Some charts look almost identical, some values are revised or contradicted between documents, and one appendix is untrusted and contains hidden instructions. Some questions are part of a conversation, and some ask your system to assume a different value for that conversation only.

The full task, rules and grading are in the Lab 2 requirements on Canvas (Part 1). This leaderboard is for practice and feedback. Final marks for Tasks 1.3 and 1.5 come from a hidden question set that graders run through your tagged Kaggle notebook after the deadline.

Team name: Pair_Programming_Team_## (your two-digit team number). Teams of two.

Evaluation
Each question is scored out of 1, and the leaderboard shows the average over all questions:

0.40 answer: the share of the required facts found in your answer. Numbers must match within 1%.
0.20 status: answered, not_found, conflict, false_premise or session_override.
0.20 figure references: F1 between your figure/table labels and the correct ones (NONE vs NONE scores 1).
0.20 citations: half for citing the right documents, half for exact citations (DOC2:p4, DOC1:Table 1-2).
The public leaderboard uses about half of the questions; the private leaderboard uses the rest.

Submission file
One row per question in Questions.csv, in the same order, with these columns:

id,conversation_id,question_id,answer,figure_references,citations,status
1,C001,Q001,"Your answer here",NONE,"DOC1:p1,DOC1:p2",answered
Join lists with commas and no spaces. Write NONE for an empty list. Check your file with python validate_submission.py submission.csv Questions.csv before submitting.
